Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: May 2025  ·  Terraform Fitness App

📱 Delete Your Account In-App

The fastest way to delete your account is directly inside the Terraform app:

  1. Open the Terraform app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab (bottom navigation bar).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account".
  4. Confirm the deletion in the two confirmation prompts that appear.
  5. Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted immediately.
⚠️ This action is immediate and irreversible. Your data cannot be recovered after deletion.

✉️ Request Deletion by Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request deletion by contacting us directly:

Email us at nchizampeni@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your Terraform account.

We will process your request and confirm deletion within 7 business days.

🗂️ What Data Is Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is immediately and permanently removed:

Data TypeAction
Account credentials (email & password)Deleted immediately
Profile information (name, height, weight, date of birth, gender)Deleted immediately
Fitness goals & progress recordsDeleted immediately
Workout sessions & exercise historyDeleted immediately
Session feedback & ratingsDeleted immediately
Progress photosDeleted immediately
Anonymised aggregate analytics (if any)May be retained

Anonymised, non-identifiable data that cannot be linked back to you may be retained for product improvement purposes in line with our Privacy Policy.

🛡️ Delete Only Your Data (Keep Account)

If you want to erase your personal data without fully deleting your account (e.g. reset all goals and history), contact us at nchizampeni@gmail.com specifying which data you would like removed.

We will process data-only requests within 7 business days and confirm what was removed.
